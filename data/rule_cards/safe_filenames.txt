RULE: Safe Filename Handling

Always use -print0 with find and -0 with xargs to handle filenames with spaces, newlines, and special characters.

CORRECT:
  find . -name "*.txt" -print0 | xargs -0 grep "pattern"
  find . -type f -print0 | while IFS= read -r -d '' f; do echo "$f"; done

INCORRECT:
  find . -name "*.txt" | xargs grep "pattern"
  for f in $(find . -type f); do echo $f; done

Always quote variable expansions: "$file" not $file
Use arrays for lists of files: files=( *.txt )
