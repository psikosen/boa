RULE: Temporary File and Directory Handling

Always use mktemp for temporary files and directories.
Always clean up with a trap.

CORRECT:
  tmpfile=$(mktemp)
  trap 'rm -f "$tmpfile"' EXIT
  echo "data" > "$tmpfile"

  tmpdir=$(mktemp -d)
  trap 'rm -rf "$tmpdir"' EXIT

INCORRECT:
  tmpfile=/tmp/myfile.txt    # race condition, not unique
  echo "data" > /tmp/output  # may conflict with other processes

For multiple temp files:
  tmpdir=$(mktemp -d)
  trap 'rm -rf "$tmpdir"' EXIT
  work="$tmpdir/work.txt"
  output="$tmpdir/output.txt"
