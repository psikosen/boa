RULE: Variable Quoting in Bash

Always double-quote variable expansions to prevent word splitting and globbing.

CORRECT:
  echo "$HOME"
  cp "$src" "$dst"
  if [ -f "$file" ]; then
  for f in "$@"; do

INCORRECT:
  echo $HOME
  cp $src $dst
  if [ -f $file ]; then
  for f in $@; do

Single quotes prevent all expansion: echo '$HOME' prints literal $HOME
Use double quotes for variables, single quotes for literal strings.
